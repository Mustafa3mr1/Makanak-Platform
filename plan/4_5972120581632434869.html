About Makanak

Makanak is a real estate platform where owners list properties and buyers or tenants submit offers, negotiate, and close with confidence. Every listing is verified and every offer is tracked from first bid to final answer.

Verified listings

Every property is reviewed by our team.

Secure offers

Offers and counter-offers stay in one history.

Three roles

Owners, buyers/tenants, and admins.
